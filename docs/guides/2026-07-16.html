How to Cook Meal Prep That You Will Actually Eat

📄 Full guide ⏱ ~5 min read 🔒 Purchased July 16, 2026

🍴 Introduction to Meal Prep

Welcome to the world of meal prep, where planning and cooking your meals in advance can save you time, money, and stress. However, let's be real – many of us have been guilty of cooking a batch of meals, only to have them sit in the fridge for days, uneaten and unloved. In this guide, we'll show you how to cook meal prep that you'll actually eat, and enjoy.

👉 Step 1: Plan Your Meals 📅

Before you start cooking, take some time to plan out your meals for the week. Consider your schedule, dietary preferences, and the ingredients you have on hand. Think about what you like to eat, and what you're in the mood for. Do you have a busy week ahead, or a relaxing weekend? This will help you determine what types of meals to prep. Make a list of the meals you want to cook, and the ingredients you'll need. You can find inspiration online, or use a meal planning app to help you get started.

🍳 Step 2: Choose Your Recipes 📚

Now that you have a plan, it's time to choose your recipes. Look for dishes that are easy to cook in bulk, and that can be reheated or eaten cold. Consider one-pot wonders, slow cooker recipes, and dishes that can be cooked in a single sheet pan. Some great options for meal prep include:

* Grilled chicken or fish with roasted vegetables

* Lentil or vegetable soups

* Salads with protein and whole grains

* Stir-fries with your favorite ingredients

* Overnight oats or breakfast burritos

👍 Step 3: Shop Smart 🛍️

Once you have your recipes, it's time to go shopping. Make a list of the ingredients you need, and try to stick to it. Buy in bulk, and look for sales or discounts on the items you need. Consider shopping at a local farmer's market, or using a meal kit delivery service to make things easier. Don't forget to shop your pantry, fridge, and freezer first – you might be surprised at what you already have on hand.

🍲 Step 4: Cook in Bulk 🍴

Now it's time to start cooking. Cook your meals in bulk, using large pots, pans, and baking sheets. This will help you save time, and make sure you have enough food for the week. Consider cooking on the weekend, or on a day off, when you have more time to focus on meal prep. Some tips for cooking in bulk include:

* Use a "cook once, eat twice" approach – cook a large batch of rice, beans, or grains, and use it throughout the week

* Cook proteins like chicken, beef, or tofu in bulk, and use them in different meals

* Roast a large batch of vegetables, and use them in salads, soups, or as a side dish

📦 Step 5: Portion and Store 📦

Once your meals are cooked, it's time to portion and store them. Use airtight containers, zip-top bags, or glass jars to keep your meals fresh. Label each container with the date, meal name, and any reheating instructions. Consider using a meal prep container or bag to make things easier. Some tips for portioning and storing include:

* Portion your meals into individual servings, to make them easy to grab and go

* Store your meals in the fridge or freezer, depending on how quickly you plan to eat them

* Use a "first in, first out" approach – eat the oldest meals first, to make sure nothing goes to waste

👌 Step 6: Reheat and Eat 🍴

The final step is to reheat and eat your meals. Make sure to follow any reheating instructions, and use a microwave, oven, or stovetop to warm up your meals. Consider adding a few fresh ingredients, like herbs, citrus, or nuts, to give your meals a boost. Some tips for reheating and eating include:

* Reheat your meals to an internal temperature of 165°F (74°C), to ensure food safety

* Add a few fresh ingredients, like a squeeze of lemon or a sprinkle of herbs, to give your meals a boost

* Eat your meals at room temperature, or chilled, if you prefer

👍 Tips and Tricks 🤔

Here are a few tips and tricks to help you succeed with meal prep:

* Start small – begin with one or two meals per week, and gradually increase the number of meals you prep

* Be flexible – don't be too hard on yourself if you don't stick to your plan. Life gets in the way, and it's okay to make changes

* Make it fun – play music, invite a friend over, or try a new recipe to make meal prep more enjoyable

* Keep it simple – don't feel like you need to make complicated meals. Simple, whole foods are often the best option

👏 Conclusion 🎉

Cooking meal prep that you'll actually eat is all about planning, cooking, and storing your meals in a way that works for you. By following these steps, and using a few simple tips and tricks, you can create delicious, healthy meals that will save you time and money. Remember to be flexible, have fun, and keep it simple. Happy cooking!
